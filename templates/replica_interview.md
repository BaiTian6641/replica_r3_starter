## The decision as it was then

1. What was happening, and what did you want to achieve?
2. What options did you actually consider?
3. What constraints existed at that time?
4. What information did you have? What did you not know?
5. What did you check or ask first, and why?
6. What did you choose?
7. What did you give up by choosing it?
8. Which factor mattered most? Was there an exception?
9. What evidence or changed constraint would have reversed your choice?

## Outcome and later interpretation

10. What actually happened afterward?
11. Which parts of your earlier judgment still seem right?
12. Which parts changed, and what evidence caused that?
13. How certain are you about the remembered details?
14. Is this a recurring pattern or an unusual decision?
