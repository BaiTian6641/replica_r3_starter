## Defect, repair and coverage

`chronological_correction` intentionally returns the complete historical row. `publish_correction` already published a compact, complete current view, but the controller passed the historical row to `generate`. The real generation method correctly rejected the mismatch with its selected canonical span. The previous completion claim therefore covered publication and orchestration, not the integration with generation.

`correction_evaluation_view` now constructs a separate minimal evaluation row from the published canonical span. It verifies the current-text digest, source epoch/identity, current record, and content-addressed full-history binding. The controller's extracted `run_correction_probe` uses that same view and authenticated batch for contextual and exact-text generation. The exact-text forward still disables memory, while both controls execute the unchanged canonical-span equality and source-validation checks. The receipt records the evaluation view and its provenance. Chronological text, original facts and revision history remain in their separate immutable artifact; no history is truncated or discarded.
