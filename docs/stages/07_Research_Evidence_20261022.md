| [SEAL v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10943v2), 18 September 2025; [authors' repository](https://github.com/Continual-Intelligence/SEAL) | Generate self-edit data/directives and reward downstream adaptation | S3 uses bounded proposals and isolated candidates. | [Voyager v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291v2), 19 October 2023 | Executable skills and feedback-driven improvement | Include a procedure-library control. Its black-box model interface does not require weight fine-tuning, so library gains and neural learning must be reported separately. |

## Initial data sources to qualify, without bulk downloading

The [PersonaMem-v2 card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bowen-upenn/PersonaMem-v2/blob/main/README.md) currently labels CC-BY-4.0, lists distinct text/multimodal train/validation/benchmark files under configuration `benchmark`, and warns that its data are LLM generated. Use training identities only for eligible mechanism training; group repeated identities across modalities. Synthetic personas cannot stand in for the user's autobiography. Pin card/data revisions and inspect inherited terms before intake.

The [LongMemEval authors' repository](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval) directs users to cleaned S/M/oracle files and tests extraction, updates, time, reasoning and abstention. These are external evaluation materials here. Oracle/evidence annotations stay evaluator-side, and shared question variants remain one statistical group. The repo's older CUDA environment is a separate reference, not a request to downgrade the local runner.

The wider catalog in [04_Datasets_and_Generation](../04_Datasets_and_Generation.md) remains a **quarantined research catalog** until source-specific revision, license, file-hash and split review. This focused review did not revalidate every row or ingest those corpora. First implement one local solver-backed adapter and one approved public adapter; keep external final benchmarks out of curriculum generation.

Neither source availability nor a unit-test pass closes those gates.
